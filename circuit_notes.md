# Circuit notes
- Supply: 9 V battery -> 7805 -> regulated 5 V for control circuitry.
- Motor switched through IRFZ44N MOSFET, PWM speed control; common ground.
- LEDs on D4-D11 (each with a series resistor), pot on A0, optional sensor on D2.
